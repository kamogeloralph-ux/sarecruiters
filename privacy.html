Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

SA Recruiters ("the app") is a free, community-maintained directory of South African recruitment agencies. This policy explains what information is collected, how it's used, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Agency directory data — agency names, phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses, websites, and the companies they recruit for. This information is submitted by app users (recruiters, agencies, or members of the public) and is publicly visible to anyone using the app. Do not submit personal information you don't want displayed publicly.
  • Admin login — a single administrator account (email and password) is used to manage the directory. This is stored securely by our database provider (Supabase) and is not accessible to other users.
  • Photos — agencies may optionally have a profile photo/logo attached, submitted by users.

We do not collect location data, device identifiers, analytics, or any information beyond what is voluntarily entered into the app's forms.

How data is used

Submitted agency information is used solely to populate the public directory so job seekers can find recruitment agencies. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose beyond the directory itself.

Data storage

All data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database provider. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Only the app administrator can edit or delete directory entries; anyone can view the directory and submit new entries.

Your choices

If you'd like an entry corrected or removed, contact us using the details below and we'll action it directly, since edits and deletions are admin-only within the app.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.
