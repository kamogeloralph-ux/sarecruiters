Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

SA Recruiters ("the app") is a free, community-maintained directory of South African recruitment agencies. Using the app requires signing in with a Google account. This policy explains what information is collected, how it's used, and your choices.

Google sign-in

You sign in with your Google account, handled by Google together with our database provider, Supabase. We receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google so the app can recognise your account. Your name and photo are shown only to you, in the site menu.

  • We never see your Google password.
  • You can sign out at any time from the site menu (or the Menu tab).

What we collect

  • Directory data — agency, branch and employer details and vacancies, submitted by users, agency managers (via private Smart Manager links) or the admin. This information is publicly visible to anyone using the app. Do not submit personal information you don't want displayed publicly.
  • Talent Pool profiles — if you join, we store your registration details (name, photo, phone number, email, sector, position, location, experience and mini-CV answers, plus an optional CV link). Only your name, photo, position, location, experience and "about me" are shown publicly; your phone number, email and CV link stay private with our team.
  • Saved vacancies — synced to your account so your saved list follows you across devices.
  • Reports & suggestions — including any contact details you optionally provide. You can see your own submissions under "My submissions".
  • Notes — private reminders you write are stored only on your device.
  • Photos — profile photos submitted for agencies, employers and Talent Pool candidates, stored by our hosting provider (Cloudflare).
  • Admin login — administrator accounts (email/password or Google) used to manage the directory. This is stored securely by Supabase and is not accessible to other users.

Location

The "Use precise location" button on directory screens asks for your device location only when you tap it. Your coordinates are turned into an area name (via a location lookup service) in your browser and used as a search filter — your precise location is never stored or sent to our database.

Email vacancy alerts

If you opt in during Talent Pool registration, we use your email address to send alerts about vacancies matching your sector and location. Every alert includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove your profile. We send no other marketing email.

Analytics

We use privacy-preserving first-party analytics. The app creates a random anonymous visitor identifier in your browser and a separate session identifier to count unique visitors, visits, vacancy views, and apply-link clicks. These identifiers do not contain your name, email address, phone number, IP address, or precise location. Authenticated administrator activity is excluded from public visitor statistics.

How data is used

Directory data is used to run the public directory; Talent Pool data to connect candidates with employers through our team; account data to sync your saved vacancies; and reports and suggestions to improve listings. Data is not sold and is not shared with advertisers. Anonymous analytics are used to understand aggregate usage and improve the app.

Data storage & third parties

  • Supabase — database, sign-in accounts and storage security.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the app, stores photos, verifies spam-protected forms and processes manager-link changes.
  • Google — the sign-in provider.
  • Location lookup service — receives your coordinates only when you tap "Use precise location".

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Smart Manager links are private tokens verified on our server — agency managers can only add to their own listing, never edit or delete.

Your choices

  • Sign out — from the site menu or Menu tab, any time.
  • Remove your Talent Pool profile — open "My Talent Pool profile" and choose Remove, or contact us.
  • Stop email alerts — use the unsubscribe link in any alert email.
  • Correct or remove a listing — contact us using the details below and we'll action it directly, since edits and deletions are admin-only within the app.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.
