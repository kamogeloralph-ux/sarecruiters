# Design direction

## Chosen direction: Editorial job-market utility
A confident, warm, high-signal vacancy browser that feels closer to a premium hiring product than a directory.

- Design movement: editorial product design with compact data density and generous whitespace.
- Core principles: search before scroll, show the next action, make comparison easy, keep details one tap away.
- Color philosophy: deep navy ink for trust, warm ivory surfaces for calm, vivid lime for active controls, coral for small moments of urgency.
- Layout paradigm: full-bleed browse header, centered content rail, desktop split between controls and listings, mobile stacked flow.
- Signature elements: oversized vacancy count, pill-like industry rail, stacked metadata rows, accent edge on featured jobs, soft borders instead of heavy shadows.
- Interaction philosophy: fast local feedback, obvious active states, keyboard-safe controls, no modal required for basic discovery.
- Animation: subtle lift and color transitions; honor prefers-reduced-motion.
- Typography system: Inter, high-contrast display sizes for page headings, compact 12–14px metadata.
- Brand essence: practical, optimistic, South African, connected.
- Brand voice: direct, useful, encouraging.
- Wordmark/logo: retain the existing SA Recruiters identity.
- Signature brand color: vivid lime accent used sparingly for the primary browse action and active industry state.
